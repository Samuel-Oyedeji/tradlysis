Backtest

Replay history through an experiment's rules
Replays months of real Capital.com candles through each experiment's strategy rules and its risk limits, with its capital as a simulated account, and shows what it would have traded. Nothing is traded or saved. Read the results as a best case: every setup counts as if the model agreed, there is no news blackout, and a 15-minute candle that touches both the stop and the target counts as a loss.
